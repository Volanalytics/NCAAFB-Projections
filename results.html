Definitions

variant
Which projection was frozen. current is the number the site showed that week; first posted is the first projection ever made for that game, never overwritten. The gap between the two rows is what the weekly refresh is worth.
n
Games graded in that row.
mgn MAE
Mean absolute error of the projected margin against the final margin. Lower is a tighter point estimate.
bias
Mean signed error, projected minus actual. Positive means the model favoured the home team too much.
SU%
Share of games where the projected winner won.
W-L-P
Wins, losses and pushes from backing whichever side the projection implies against the posted number.
ROI
Profit divided by settled bets, at the price actually offered. Pushes leave the divisor rather than counting as losses.
CLV
Closing line value: for spreads and totals, points of line gained between the bet and the last capture before kickoff; for moneylines, the change in no-vig win probability. Positive means the market moved toward the side that was taken.
beat
Share of bets with positive CLV. Over a season this settles whether there is an edge here long before the win-loss record does.
no-vig
The book's two prices converted to probabilities and rescaled to sum to 1, removing the margin built into the quote.
edge
How far the projection sat from the posted number, in points, when the bet was priced.
cross-division
One FBS team against one FCS team. Held-out validation on FBS-only games never saw these, and they carried a large bias in 2026 Week 1, so they get their own row here permanently.
Spread and total bets are recorded whenever the disagreement clears 0 point(s); moneyline bets need 3 percentage points of edge over the no-vig price. Every bet is 1 unit at the offered price, with a missing price treated as -110. Model output, not advice.
